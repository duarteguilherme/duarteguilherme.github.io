Guilherme Duarte

I’m Guilherme Duarte, an assistant professor in the Department of Government at Harvard University.

My research focuses on causal inference and machine learning. I develop algorithms for partial identification and study the sensitivity of causal conclusions to violations of assumptions. I also work on transportability and factorial experiments.

gjduarte@fas.harvard.edu · CV

Background

I received my Ph.D. from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania in 2025. Before academia, I worked in journalism and data science.

My work has received a Pulitzer Prize for Explanatory Reporting (2017), the Society for Political Methodology’s Best Poster Award (2023), John T. Williams Dissertation Prize (2024), and Gosnell Prize (2026), and the Society for Causal Inference’s Tom Ten Have Award (2024).
